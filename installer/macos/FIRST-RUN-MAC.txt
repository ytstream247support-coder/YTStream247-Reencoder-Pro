YT Stream 247 — Reencoder - First Run on macOS
=================================================

HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
1. Drag "YT Stream 247 — Reencoder" into the Applications folder
   (shortcut on the right side of this window).
2. Close this window and eject the disk image from Finder.


HOW TO OPEN THE FIRST TIME
==========================
macOS will block the app the first time because it is not signed with an
Apple Developer ID. This is expected and safe.

The exact steps depend on your macOS version. Find your version in the
Apple menu -> About This Mac, then follow the matching section below.


-------------------------------------------------------------
FOR macOS 14 (Sonoma) AND macOS 13 (Ventura)
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Applications folder.
2. RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) on "YT Stream 247 — Reencoder".
3. Choose "Open" from the menu.
4. A dialog appears -> click the "Open" button.
5. Done. The app launches and will open normally next time.


-------------------------------------------------------------
FOR macOS 15 (Sequoia) AND macOS 26 (Tahoe)
-------------------------------------------------------------
On these versions Apple removed the "Open" button from the first dialog.
You will see only "Move to Trash" and "Done". Do NOT click Move to Trash.

1. Open the Applications folder.
2. Try to open "YT Stream 247 — Reencoder" (double-click or right-click ->
   Open). A dialog says "Apple could not verify ... is free of malware".
3. Click "Done" to close the dialog.
4. Open System Settings (Apple menu -> System Settings).
5. Click "Privacy & Security" in the left sidebar.
6. Scroll the right pane all the way down.
7. You will see a message:
      "YT Stream 247 — Reencoder" was blocked to protect your Mac.
   Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
8. Enter your Mac password when asked.
9. Go back to Applications and open the app.
10. A new dialog appears with an "Open" button -> click "Open".
11. Done. The app launches and will open normally next time.


-------------------------------------------------------------
EASY METHOD (ALL macOS VERSIONS) - For users comfortable with Terminal
-------------------------------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications -> Utilities -> Terminal) and paste this
command, then press Enter:

    xattr -cr "/Applications/YT Stream 247 — Reencoder.app"

After this, just double-click the app normally. No dialogs at all.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
- macOS 13 (Ventura) or later
- Works on both Intel and Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4) Macs
- No additional software required - FFmpeg is bundled inside the app


WHY DO I SEE THIS WARNING?
--------------------------
We have not yet purchased an Apple Developer ID code-signing certificate
($99/year). macOS treats unsigned apps as "unverified". The app itself is
safe - the warning is purely about who signed it, not what the app does.


SUPPORT

If you still have problems after following the steps above, contact

- Whether your Mac is Intel or Apple Silicon
- A description of the problem and any error message
